# DfCM

Design for Combinatorial Maximalism preserves reversible candidate space before selection.

1. derive constraints from admitted contracts;
2. enumerate known patterns, tools, compositions, and variants;
3. manufacture candidate realizations and candidate falsifiers;
4. qualify candidates independently;
5. delay irreversible selection until evidence justifies it;
6. promote successful novel mechanisms into reusable manufacturing knowledge.

Do not optimize for the fewest artifacts. Optimize for the largest lawful, economically qualifiable design frontier.
