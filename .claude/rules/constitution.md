# APS Constitutional Rule

Applies to the entire repository except `archive/`.

- Everything is sunk at a new decision boundary.
- Preserve truth, not implementations.
- Zero continuation privilege; zero uninformed elimination.
- Contract before implementation.
- Known pattern before novel mechanism.
- ggen-first: if a specialized generator is superior, manufacture its declarations and invocation upstream.
- SELECT != CONSTRUCT != DO.
- No ambient DO authority.
- Zero unreceipted actuation.
- No prose outranks observed evidence.
- The factory itself must remain reconstitutable.
