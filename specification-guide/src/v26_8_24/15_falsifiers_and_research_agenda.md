# Falsifiers and Research Agenda

APS v26.8.24 is intentionally a candidate constitution. Its strongest claims must remain falsifiable.

## Crown hypothesis

The long-horizon hypothesis is:

> A sufficiently complete semantic manufacturing system can make hand-authored enterprise code cease to be the durable carrier of enterprise knowledge, while preserving or improving authority, safety, auditability, interoperability, and process fitness.

This is not `ALIVE` merely because component technologies exist.

## Required falsifier families

### 1. Semantic closure

Can public ontology + enterprise profile + operational constraints specify enough of a real enterprise capability to manufacture an executable realization without hidden human semantic decisions?

Failure conditions include ambiguity, invented meaning, uncontrolled local extensions, and semantic drift between operational and public views.

### 2. Jig completeness

Can a new operator or autonomous system begin from an objective and unambiguously discover the workpiece, datum, authority, legal operations, next transition, qualification route, and standing?

If repository archaeology or inventor memory is required, the relevant jig claim fails.

### 3. Manufacturing fixed point

Can the manufacturing system reproduce or reconstitute its own required manufacturing knowledge and prove bounded fixed-point properties where determinism is claimed?

### 4. Generator/tool composition

Can known external generators and executables be safely driven by manufactured declarations and invocations without silently transferring ambient authority to those tools?

### 5. Semantic-operational round trip

For representative AshR2RML-style cases:

```text
RDF/SHACL -> generated application -> relational state -> R2RML/SPARQL
```

must preserve admitted semantic identity, datatype, cardinality, and relationships within the stated profile.

### 6. Heterogeneous execution parity

Native, generated, and wrapped executables must produce comparable invocation/evidence structures without claiming semantic equivalence that has not been proven.

### 7. Process intelligence fitness

The enterprise event corpus must support conformance, variant, bottleneck, performance, and fitness analysis across real heterogeneous processes rather than only toy workflows.

### 8. Governance compression

Demonstrate that governance effort can be concentrated on source/manufacturing/verifier law while artifact count grows combinatorially, without hiding risk in unreviewed generator behavior.

### 9. Adversarial liability

For safety- or liability-relevant scenarios, reconstruct years later:

- what was known;
- what was admitted;
- what policy applied;
- what candidate was selected;
- what entity possessed authority;
- what actually executed;
- what evidence was produced;
- what verifier assigned standing.

A system that ends the explanation at 'the AI decided' fails.

### 10. Reconstitution and sunset

Given a nontrivial incumbent system, recover observable contract, manufacture a successor, independently establish required equivalence/fitness, coexist where necessary, and produce a defensible sunset decision.

### 11. Enterprise remanufacture economics

Measure actual human-equivalent lifecycle effort avoided across implementation, verification, deployment, integration, documentation, evidence, and coordination. Do not use generated LOC alone as proof of economic value.

### 12. Failure of the paradigm itself

APS must admit evidence that the paradigm is wrong or bounded. Candidate refutations include:

- irrecoverable tacit knowledge remains dominant at enterprise scale;
- semantic admission costs exceed downstream translation savings;
- generator qualification costs scale nearly linearly with artifacts;
- public ontology alignment creates more complexity than it removes;
- process evidence cannot support required causal/fitness decisions;
- reconstitution risk remains greater than incumbent modification for most consequential systems.

## Required evidence style

Prefer:

- exact coordinates;
- real subprocesses/services/databases where the claim concerns them;
- state-based Chicago tests;
- sabotage and negative fixtures;
- independent verifiers;
- deterministic replay where claimed;
- public standards and ontologies rather than private semantic invention where appropriate;
- explicit residual gaps.

## Research program

The immediate program is not to defend the current implementation estate. It is to construct progressively stronger jigs and execute adversarial enterprise reconstitution experiments until the architecture either earns the claimed standing or exposes its limits.

The success condition is not universal adoption. The Fuller-style condition is stronger: build a successor production model whose qualified consequences make the previous human translation system economically unnecessary where the evidence supports that conclusion.
