# Autonomic Manufacturing Manifesto

APS v26.8.24 names a production model in which public semantics, executable contracts, deterministic/replayable manufacture, explicit authority, independent qualification, and process intelligence combine to remanufacture knowledge-work systems continuously.

Software is the first major substrate. The wider object is **autonomic knowledge-work manufacturing**.

## The manifesto

1. **Everything is sunk at the next decision boundary.** Historical production cost creates no continuation privilege.
2. **Preserve truth, not implementations.** Recover behavior, contracts, evidence, and obligations; replace machinery freely.
3. **Zero continuation privilege does not mean uninformed deletion.** Observe before sunset.
4. **Public semantics outrank private duplication where a suitable public vocabulary exists.** Enterprise facts and operational profiles add bounded local commitment.
5. **Contract before implementation.** Design the law that implementations must satisfy.
6. **A = mu(O*).** Artifacts are consequences of admitted knowledge and manufacturing law.
7. **Known pattern? Compose it.** Reuse accumulated executable knowledge.
8. **Known tool? Generate its invocation.** Specialized generators and executables are capital equipment downstream of manufacturing authority.
9. **Novel mechanism? Discover it once, then teach the factory.** Novelty should compound into reusable capital.
10. **Applications are projections.** Their durable meaning should not be trapped in current source, database, framework, SaaS vendor, or deployment topology.
11. **Libraries retain irreducible mechanism; their surrounding surfaces remain manufacturable.**
12. **DfCM preserves candidate space before selection.** Maximize lawful reversible options under resource, evidence, and authority bounds.
13. **Adversaries manufacture requirements.** A credible objection becomes a candidate falsifier and, when admitted, a reusable qualification gate.
14. **SELECT is not CONSTRUCT; CONSTRUCT is not DO.** Planning and generation confer no ambient consequence authority.
15. **Zero unreceipted actuation.** Consequence without reconstructable evidence has no APS standing.
16. **Process conformance is not process fitness.** Full process intelligence must test whether the authorized process itself remains safe and economically fit.
17. **Governance scales with law, not artifact count.** Validate versioned source, contract, manufacturing, authority, and verifier classes; automatically qualify their consequences.
18. **Software manufacturing capital is reconstitutable.** The factory receives no permanent exemption from Chatman's Law.
19. **Code becomes inventory when it is reproducible.** Durable capital migrates toward semantic, contractual, manufacturing, and evidentiary knowledge.
20. **No prose outranks evidence.** The strongest thesis remains a hypothesis until exact observations earn standing.

## Terminal direction

The long-run target is not 'AI writes code faster.' That can remain automated craftsmanship.

The target is:

```text
enterprise intent / reality
  -> public semantics + enterprise facts
  -> executable contracts
  -> DfCM manufacturing space
  -> ggen-first construction + specialized capital equipment
  -> qualified applications / bindings / infrastructure / tests / docs
  -> explicit authority
  -> consequential execution
  -> object-centric process evidence
  -> independent verification
  -> standing
  -> reusable learning
  -> next reconstitution
```

When that loop closes for a domain, implementation becomes increasingly disposable while knowledge becomes cumulative.
