# Contributing to APS

Contributions are reconstitution proposals, not preservation requests.

## Required shape

A material proposal should identify:

- predecessor/reality observations;
- admitted semantic subjects and constraints;
- executable contract changes;
- candidate manufacturing strategies;
- candidate falsifiers;
- authority implications;
- independent qualification route;
- process-evidence implications;
- sunset/reconstitution consequences;
- residual `UNKNOWN` or unsupported boundaries.

Prefer public standards and ontologies to project-private duplication when they express the required semantics.

## Implementation law

Known pattern? Compose it. Known tool? Generate its invocation. Novel mechanism? Discover it once, qualify it, and make it a candidate reusable manufacturing primitive.

Generated output must not become a second source of truth. When generated artifacts are needed for distribution, build them in CI or a release boundary and bind them to the admitted source coordinate.

## Validation

```bash
make verify
```

A PR should be judged at its exact head. Green evidence on an ancestor is not promotion evidence for the current candidate.
